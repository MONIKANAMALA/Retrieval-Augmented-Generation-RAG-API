# Acme Corp Company Policies

## Refund Policy

All customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refund requests must be submitted through the customer portal or by contacting customer support at support@acmecorp.example.com. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval. Products must be returned in their original packaging and in unused condition. Digital products and subscription services are eligible for a prorated refund within the first 14 days only. After the 30-day window, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances.

## Shipping Policy

Standard shipping is available for all domestic orders and takes 5-7 business days. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee of $12.99. International shipping is available to select countries and takes 10-15 business days. Free shipping is provided on all orders over $75. All orders are shipped with tracking numbers provided via email within 24 hours of shipment. Acme Corp is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing for international orders. Fragile items require special handling and may incur additional shipping charges of up to $8.00.

## Cancellation Policy

Orders may be cancelled within 2 hours of placement for a full refund. After 2 hours, orders that have not yet shipped may be cancelled with a 10% restocking fee. Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled and must follow the standard return and refund process. Subscription services may be cancelled at any time, with the cancellation taking effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No partial refunds are given for mid-cycle cancellations of subscription services. To cancel an order, contact customer support with your order number and reason for cancellation.

## Privacy Policy

Acme Corp collects only the personal information necessary to process orders and provide customer support. Customer data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption and stored on secure servers within the United States. We do not sell or share customer data with third parties for marketing purposes. Customers may request a full copy of their stored data or request deletion by contacting privacy@acmecorp.example.com. Data retention policy requires that inactive customer records are automatically purged after 24 months.

## Payment Terms

Acme Corp accepts Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and PayPal. All prices are listed in US Dollars (USD). Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping destination and applied at checkout. Corporate accounts with approved credit may be eligible for net-30 payment terms. Late payments on corporate accounts incur a 1.5% monthly interest charge.
