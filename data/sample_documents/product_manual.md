# AcmeWidget Pro X200 - Product Manual

## Product Overview

The AcmeWidget Pro X200 is a premium multi-function device designed for both home and office use. Model number: AW-X200-PRO. The device weighs 2.4 kg and has dimensions of 30cm x 20cm x 8cm. Operating temperature range is 5°C to 40°C. Power supply: 110-240V AC, 50/60Hz. Maximum power consumption is 45 watts. The device includes a 5-inch LCD display with touch controls and supports Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) and Bluetooth 5.2 connectivity.

## Setup Instructions

1. Remove the device from its packaging and verify all components are present: main unit, power adapter, USB-C cable, and quick start guide.
2. Place the device on a flat, stable surface with adequate ventilation (minimum 10cm clearance on all sides).
3. Connect the power adapter to the device and plug it into a grounded outlet.
4. Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds until the LED indicator turns solid blue.
5. Follow the on-screen setup wizard to configure Wi-Fi connectivity and user preferences.
6. Download the AcmeWidget Companion App from the App Store or Google Play to enable remote monitoring and control.
7. Register your device at register.acmecorp.example.com to activate your warranty.

## Troubleshooting

### Device Does Not Power On
- Verify the power adapter is securely connected to both the device and the outlet.
- Try a different power outlet.
- Check that the outlet is providing power by testing with another device.
- If the LED indicator flashes red, the internal battery may need replacement. Contact support.

### Wi-Fi Connection Issues
- Ensure your router is broadcasting on a supported frequency (2.4GHz or 5GHz).
- Move the device closer to your router.
- Restart both the device and your router.
- Verify the Wi-Fi password was entered correctly during setup.
- For enterprise networks, WPA2-Enterprise requires additional configuration through the admin portal.

### Display Shows Error Code E-401
- Error code E-401 indicates a sensor calibration failure.
- Power cycle the device by unplugging it for 30 seconds.
- If the error persists, perform a factory reset by holding the reset button (located on the bottom panel) for 10 seconds.
- Contact technical support if the issue continues after a factory reset.

### Unusual Noise During Operation
- A slight humming noise during normal operation is expected and not cause for concern.
- Loud clicking or grinding noises may indicate a mechanical issue. Stop using the device and contact support immediately.
- Ensure the device is on a flat surface and not touching other objects that could cause vibration.

## Maintenance

Clean the exterior of the device with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners, solvents, or spray liquids directly on the device. The air intake filters should be cleaned every 3 months by removing them and rinsing with clean water. Allow filters to dry completely before reinstalling. Firmware updates are delivered automatically when the device is connected to Wi-Fi. Manual firmware updates can be performed by downloading the latest version from support.acmecorp.example.com and installing via USB-C.
