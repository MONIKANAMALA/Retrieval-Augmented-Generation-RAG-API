# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month of service. New employees may begin using accrued leave after completing their 90-day probationary period. Unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal. Leave requests during peak business periods (November 15 through January 15) require manager and director approval. Employees separating from the company will be paid out for unused accrued leave.

## Remote Work Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work arrangements must be documented in a Remote Work Agreement signed by the employee and their direct manager. Employees working remotely must maintain a dedicated workspace that meets ergonomic standards. The company provides a one-time $500 home office setup stipend for approved remote workers. Remote employees are required to be available during core business hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone. All remote workers must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Remote work privileges may be revoked if performance standards are not maintained.

## Sick Leave

Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over between calendar years and unused days are forfeited on December 31st. Absences of 3 or more consecutive days require a medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. Employees must notify their manager of sick leave before 9:00 AM on the day of absence. Extended illness beyond available sick leave may qualify for short-term disability benefits after a 14-day waiting period.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually in June and December. Each review evaluates employees on five core competencies: technical skills, teamwork, communication, initiative, and reliability. Employees who receive an "Exceeds Expectations" rating are eligible for a merit increase of up to 8%. A "Needs Improvement" rating initiates a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). Employees who successfully complete their PIP return to standard review cycles.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior in all workplace interactions. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying of any kind are strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary action. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR within 30 days of becoming aware of the potential conflict. Use of company resources for personal gain is prohibited. Violation of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Benefits Overview

Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance (medical, dental, and vision) starting on the first day of the month following their hire date. The company contributes 80% of premium costs for employee coverage and 50% for dependent coverage. A 401(k) retirement plan is available with a company match of up to 4% of the employee's salary. Life insurance equal to 2x annual salary is provided at no cost to the employee. An Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provides free confidential counseling services.
